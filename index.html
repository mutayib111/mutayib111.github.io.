How I work

From friction to a useful tool

This is the mindset I would bring to an AI-in-design workflow: understand the task first, then test the simplest useful intervention.

STEP 01

Understand

Map the current process and identify repetitive, slow, or error-prone steps.

STEP 02

Explore

Compare AI tools and conventional automation against a clear task and success criteria.

STEP 03

Prototype

Build a small script or interface and test it on representative examples.

STEP 04

Document

Record limitations, usage steps, output quality, and what should be improved next.
